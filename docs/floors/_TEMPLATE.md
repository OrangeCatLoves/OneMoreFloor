## Goal (one line)
The line shown on the title card. Example: "Get out before the building comes down."

## How it plays
3–6 sentences: what players see and do, from the doors opening to the floor being cleared.

## Cleared when
The exact rule. Example: every player reaches the exit.

## Costs hearts
| Mistake | Hearts lost | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| e.g. hit by falling debris | 1 | respawn at the last checkpoint after 2 s |

## Stops stalling
What keeps costing hearts if the team stops making progress? Example: when the collapse timer hits 0, everyone still inside loses 1 heart and the floor resets.

## Gets harder per level
| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1 | the starting version |
| 2 | e.g. collapse timer 10 s shorter |
| 3 | e.g. more debris, fewer checkpoints |

## Solo vs co-op (1–4 players)
What changes with more players, if anything. Who must finish? What do players who finish early do?

## Controls
